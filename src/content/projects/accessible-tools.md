A mobile-first toolkit for people who struggle with tiny buttons: elderly users, people with special needs, and workers whose hands are busy or dirty (cooks, plumbers, construction workers).

- **Calculator** with cooking (fractions) and construction (feet/inches) modes
- **Notes** with voice dictation and read-aloud
- **Recipes** that scale from ½× to 3×, with a hands-free cook mode that reads each step aloud
- **Converter** that shows every relevant unit at once

64px minimum touch targets, four global font sizes, light / dark / high-contrast themes. It works offline as an installable PWA, with no account and all data kept on the device.
